# Research Note: Australian Specialty Flour Suppliers

## Question

Which Australian suppliers currently look useful for harder-to-get flours and
grains such as emmer, einkorn, khorasan, barley flour, sorghum, millet,
kibbled rye, pea flour, and potato flour?

## Short answer

The strongest current supplier map looks like this: `The Grain Family` is the
best Tasmania-first option for fresh-milled local ancient grains and even blue
barley, but it generally prefers local distribution and mainland shipping is
not their default. `Four Leaf Milling`, `Simply No Knead`, and `Honest to
Goodness` look like the most useful broad Australian online retail options.
`Risen Flour` is especially relevant for emmer and einkorn, though einkorn
looks limited and therefore likely volatile. `Bio-Oz` matters as an Australian
ancient-wheat processor, while `Mirfak` has the broadest technical range for
barley, millet, sorghum, and pea flours but appears to be aimed more at
wholesale trade than ordinary home-baker shopping.

## Ingredient-specific notes

### Emmer

- `The Grain Family` currently lists emmer flour and grain.
- `SNK` currently lists emmer flour.
- `Bio-Oz` states it has Australian-grown emmer flour available.
- `Risen Flour` currently lists Australian-grown stoneground emmer flour.

### Einkorn

- `Risen Flour` is the clearest Australian hit I found for einkorn flour.
- Availability appears limited; the current page showed it as sold out at crawl
  time, which suggests einkorn should be treated as a watch item rather than a
  stable pantry staple.

### Khorasan

- `The Grain Family` currently lists khorasan flour and grain.
- `SNK` lists both white and whole khorasan options.
- `Honest to Goodness` lists sustainable white khorasan flour.
- `Bio-Oz` states it has Australian-grown khorasan flour available.
- `Goodness Flour` lists khorasan flour sourced from The Grain Family in
  Tasmania.

### Barley flour

- `The Grain Family` lists Tibetan blue barley flour.
- `Four Leaf Milling` lists barley flour.
- `Mirfak` lists both wholemeal barley flour and barley flour in its product
  range.

### Sorghum flour

- `Honest to Goodness` currently lists Australian white sorghum flour.
- `Goodness Flour` also lists sorghum flour.
- `Mirfak` lists both white and red sorghum flour.

### Millet flour

- `The Source Bulk Foods` currently lists Australian organic millet flour.
- `Four Leaf Milling` lists millet flour.
- `Mirfak` lists millet flour as well.

### Kibbled rye

- `Four Leaf Milling` sells cracked rye and explicitly notes it is also known
  as kibbled rye.
- `SNK` currently lists kibbled rye, though the page was backordered at crawl
  time.
- `The Bake and Brew Shop` also currently lists kibbled rye for retail sale.

### Pea flour

- `Goodness Flour` currently lists yellow pea flour.
- `Mirfak` lists both yellow pea flour and green pea flour.

### Potato flour

- Clean Australian sourcing for true whole potato flour looks less settled than
  for the grain flours.
- Many Australian listings are for `potato starch`, not `potato flour`, and
  those are not interchangeable.
- `Natural Evolution Foods` is a strong Australian source for gold sweet potato
  flour, but that is a different ingredient from standard potato flour.

## Practical shopping strategy

1. Start with `The Grain Family` for Tasmania-local ancient grains and special
   wholegrain flours.
2. Use `Four Leaf Milling` and `SNK` as the main national specialty backup.
3. Use `Honest to Goodness` for easier national ordering of sorghum and
   khorasan.
4. Use `Risen Flour` when specifically chasing emmer or einkorn.
5. Treat `Mirfak` as a catalogue and possible supply lead when hunting unusual
   pulse or minor-grain flours at larger pack sizes.
6. Be cautious with `potato flour` labels and verify they refer to whole potato
   flour rather than starch.
