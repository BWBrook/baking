# Research

This directory stores concise, sourced research notes.

Use it for questions such as:

- ideal hydration ranges for a flour blend,
- Dutch oven timing and temperature choices,
- rye handling and proofing behavior,
- banneton preparation and rice flour use,
- differences between instant yeast and levain workflows.

Each research note should include:

- the question being investigated,
- the date of research,
- a short synthesis in plain language,
- source links,
- practical implications for this repository.

Keep research notes concise. Save conclusions, not just collected links.
