# Oven and Vessel Notes

This note records the current practical baseline for baking setup in this
kitchen.

## Oven

- Main oven: built-in electric fan-forced oven.
- Normal setting: fan-forced bake using top and bottom elements plus fan.
- Default shelf: middle shelf.
- Practical temperature rule: reduce many non-fan recipe temperatures by about
  10 C to 20 C when adapting them for this oven.
- Optional finish: for some breads, use a short high grill finish of about 2 to
  3 minutes to improve top browning.

## Dutch oven

- About 10 inches across.
- Round.
- Deep, around 5 L.
- Comfortably handles the house-standard yeasted loaf at 750 g total flour,
  even after oven spring.
- Default vessel for yeasted hearth breads.

## Cast-iron skillet

- About 10 inches across.
- Round.
- Lid is close fitting and seals steam well.
- Default vessel for quick breads in the 500 g to 650 g total-flour range.

## Shape implications

- Because the Dutch oven and skillet are both round, boules and round quick
  breads are currently the easiest default forms.
- Tin loaves are not part of the standard setup yet because there is no loaf
  tin on hand.

## Tray and open-bake options

- There is no pizza stone or baking stone at present.
- A baking tray is still a practical path for baguettes, biscuits, scones, and
  other open-baked items.
- Steam-tray baking is a possible future method for tray-baked breads, but has
  not yet been tested in this kitchen.

## Practical implications

- Recipe temperatures from books or online sources should not be followed
  blindly if they assume a conventional non-fan oven.
- Covered baking is a normal and reliable part of the house workflow.
- Both main covered vessels are now well enough defined that future bake logs
  should reference them directly.
- When suggesting loaf shapes, prefer round formats unless the recipe clearly
  belongs on a tray or in a tin.
