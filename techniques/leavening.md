# Leavening Notes

This note captures the current leavening baseline and future directions.

## Current working baseline

### Yeasted doughs

- Default method: instant dry yeast.
- This is the simplest current house standard for loaf development.
- Recipes should assume this unless explicitly marked otherwise.

### Chemically leavened baking

- Default method: baking powder.
- Baking soda is available, but is not yet the default system.

## Future directions

### Sourdough starter and levain

- Not yet the default.
- Worth exploring later once the repo has a stronger yeast-bread baseline.

### Baking soda plus cream of tartar

- Potential future path for some quick breads, flatbreads, or emergency
  baking-powder substitutions.
- Cream of tartar is not yet listed in inventory and should not be assumed.

### Unleavened breads

- Flatbreads and other unleavened styles are explicitly in scope for future
  work.
- These should be treated as a separate method family, not as failed yeasted
  breads.

## Good questions for later research

- When does instant yeast outperform natural leavening for the kinds of breads
  baked in this kitchen?
- Which flour types here are most suitable for sourdough experiments?
- What fan-forced oven adjustments are most reliable for covered versus
  uncovered loaf baking?
