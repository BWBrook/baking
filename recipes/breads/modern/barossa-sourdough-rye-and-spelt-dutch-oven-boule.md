# Barossa Sourdough Rye and Spelt Dutch-Oven Boule

## Goal

Turn one `600 g` Laucke Barossa Sourdough Rye premix into a single `750 g`
house boule by adding `150 g` wholemeal spelt flour, then baking it in the
Dutch oven rather than as a tin loaf.

## Source basis

This recipe is based on:

- the Laucke product page,
- the packet ingredient panel,
- the packet's hand-kneaded formula and method.

The manufacturer hand-kneaded method for one `600 g` pack is:

- `600 g` bread mix,
- `390 ml` water,
- `2 tsp` yeast,
- vigorous kneading,
- one bulk rise to about double,
- degas, shape into an oiled tin,
- second rise to at least double,
- bake at `220 C conventional / 200 C fan-forced` for `25 to 30` minutes.

This house version keeps the same basic system but adapts it to:

- `600 g` mix plus `150 g` wholemeal spelt flour,
- one banneton-proofed boule,
- Dutch-oven baking in the fan-forced electric oven,
- cautious water control for a shape-holding loaf.

## Mix notes

The premix already includes:

- unbleached wheat flour,
- rye flour (`27%`),
- dry wheat sourdough (`3%`),
- wheat gluten,
- non-iodised salt,
- malt flour (`wheat`),
- inactive dry yeast,
- ascorbic acid,
- enzymes,
- added vitamins.

The yeast sachet includes:

- dry yeast,
- emulsifier (`491`).

That means this is a commercial sourdough-style premix, not a naturally
leavened pure sourdough formula. It already contains salt and improvers, so the
recipe does not add any extra salt, sugar, or fat.

## Formula

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laucke Barossa Sourdough Rye Bread Mix | 600 g | One full internal flour bag |
| Wholemeal spelt flour | 150 g | House addition |
| Instant dry yeast | 2 tsp | Or use the included yeast sachet for one `600 g` pack |
| Water | 480 to 500 g | Start at `480 g`, then adjust carefully |
| Rice flour or flour for dusting | as needed | Best for banneton release |

## Hydration guidance

The manufacturer uses `390 ml` water for the plain `600 g` mix. Adding `150 g`
wholemeal spelt flour pushes the dough toward needing roughly another `90 g` to
`110 g` water, but a Dutch-oven boule also needs enough structure to hold
shape.

House guidance:

- start with `480 g` water,
- mix fully,
- rest `10 to 15` minutes,
- then add more water only in small increments if the dough still feels too
  tight.

Target texture:

- soft,
- tacky,
- elastic,
- slightly sticky to the hands,
- able to hold a rounded shape after development,
- not slack enough to puddle.

## Method

1. Mix the dry ingredients. Combine the `600 g` premix, the `150 g` wholemeal
   spelt flour, and the yeast in a large bowl.
2. Add `480 g` lukewarm water. The packet guidance aims for water around `30 C`,
   which is still a good target here.
3. Mix until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest `10 to 15` minutes so the rye
   and spelt can hydrate.
4. Reassess hydration. If the dough feels noticeably too stiff, add more water
   `10 g` at a time until it becomes soft and tacky but still manageable.
5. Knead vigorously for about `8 to 10` minutes, aiming for a smooth, elastic
   dough. Because this loaf includes rye and spelt, do not obsess over an
   extreme white-bread windowpane, but it should still show good cohesion and a
   decent stretch.
6. Return the dough to a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let it rise until
   roughly doubled and clearly lively. The packet suggests about `40` minutes;
   in practice `40 to 60` minutes is a more useful house range.
7. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured bench and degas it gently.
8. Pre-shape into a round and rest `10` minutes.
9. Final-shape into a taut boule and place seam-side up in a well-floured
   banneton.
10. Final-proof until the loaf is clearly fuller and a floured finger poke
    springs back slowly. Do not insist on a dramatic full double in the
    banneton if the dough is already well aerated.
11. Preheat the Dutch oven and lid in the oven at `240 C` fan-forced for `30 to
    45` minutes.
12. Turn the loaf out, score as desired, and load into the Dutch oven.
13. Bake covered for `20` minutes at `230 C` fan-forced.
14. Remove the lid, reduce to `210 C` fan-forced, and bake for a further `20 to
    25` minutes until well coloured and about `96 C` internal.
15. Cool fully before slicing.

## Baking setup

- Total flour target: `750 g`
- Oven style: electric fan-forced oven
- Default shelf: middle shelf
- Vessel: preheated Dutch oven
- Preheat: `240 C` fan-forced for `30 to 45` minutes
- Bake temperatures: `230 C` covered, then `210 C` uncovered
- Covered or uncovered phases: `20` minutes covered, `20 to 25` minutes
  uncovered

## Result targets

- Crumb: soft, even, and sliceable
- Crust: golden-brown and moderately crisp
- Flavor: mild rye, gentle sourdough note, slight sweetness from the wheat and
  spelt
- Shape: rounded boule with decent height and a creamy-tan crumb

## Variables to watch

- Whether the dough is happiest closer to `480 g` or `500 g` water
- Whether the added spelt makes the loaf proof faster than expected
- Whether a slightly shorter final proof gives better oven spring
- Whether the crust is best with the full uncovered bake or a slightly shorter
  finish

## Notes

- The added wholemeal spelt is a house customization, not part of the
  manufacturer's formula.
- This premix is designed to give sourdough character through dried sourdough
  and commercial yeast, not through a live starter.
- Because the mix already contains salt and improvers, avoid casual extra
  additions until the base house version has been tested.
