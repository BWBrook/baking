# Gluten-Free Thirded Skillet Quick Bread

## Goal

Create a gluten-free equal-thirds quick bread using medium-grind cornmeal,
quinoa flour, and buckwheat flour, baked in the cast-iron skillet and supported
with psyllium husk for better binding.

## Concept note

This is not a historical `thirded bread` reconstruction. It is a modern
quick-bread riff built on the equal-thirds idea:

- one third cornmeal,
- one third quinoa flour,
- one third buckwheat flour.

It is designed to suit the current pantry, the skillet workflow, and a
gluten-free structure that still slices reasonably well.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium-grind cornmeal | 180 g | Main texture and corn flavor |
| Quinoa flour | 180 g | Adds body and earthy nuttiness |
| Buckwheat flour | 180 g | Adds depth and a darker grain note |
| Psyllium husk | 12 g | Binding support |
| Sugar | 20 g | Optional but recommended for balance |
| Baking powder | 16 g | Main leavener |
| Baking soda | 4 g | Supports lift with the yogurt |
| Fine salt | 10 g |  |
| Greek yogurt | 250 g | Pantry-friendly acidity and moisture |
| Water | 280 g | Add a little more only if batter is too stiff |
| Eggs | 3 large | About `150 g` out of shell |
| Olive oil | 70 g | Default fat |
| Extra oil for skillet | as needed | For greasing |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to `185 C` fan-forced. Lightly oil the `10"` cast-iron
   skillet.
2. In a large bowl whisk together the cornmeal, quinoa flour, buckwheat flour,
   psyllium husk, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. In a second bowl whisk together the Greek yogurt, water, eggs, and olive
   oil until smooth.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir thoroughly until no
   dry pockets remain. The batter should be thick and spoonable rather than
   pourable.
5. Rest the batter `10` minutes so the cornmeal and psyllium can hydrate.
6. Reassess consistency. If the batter has become too stiff to spread
   comfortably, add `10 g` to `20 g` extra water.
7. Transfer the batter to the skillet and smooth the top.
8. Bake on the middle shelf for `35 to 42` minutes, until the top is set and a
   toothpick inserted in the centre comes out mostly clean.
9. Cool in the skillet for at least `15` minutes before slicing. This bread
   firms as it cools.

## Baking setup

- Tin, tray, skillet, or other vessel: `10"` cast-iron skillet with lid kept
  off
- Oven setting: fan-forced bake
- Temperature: `185 C`
- Time: `35 to 42` minutes

## Result targets

- Texture: moist, hearty, and slightly coarse in a good way
- Flavor: nutty, earthy, and distinctly grain-forward
- Structure: holds slices together better than a binder-free gluten-free loaf
- Appearance: bronzed top with a darker, grain-speckled crumb

## Variables to watch

- Whether `12 g` psyllium is the right binding level
- Whether the quinoa note feels pleasantly nutty or too assertive
- Whether the batter wants the extra `10 g` to `20 g` water after resting
- Whether a slightly longer cooling time improves slicing further

## Notes

- Psyllium is intentional here, not optional decoration. It should materially
  improve sliceability and reduce crumbling.
- Greek yogurt helps both tenderness and leavening support.
- If the quinoa flavour proves too strong, a later version could shift the
  thirds slightly while still keeping the same general concept.
