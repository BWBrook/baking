# Skillet Cornbread with White Flour

## Goal

Adapt the Bob's Red Mill medium-grind cornmeal packet recipe into a house
version for the `10"` cast-iron skillet, replacing the gluten-free flour blend
with ordinary white flour and scaling the batter to suit the skillet better.

## Source basis

The packet recipe is:

- `1 1/2 cups` gluten-free 1-to-1 baking flour,
- `1 1/4 cups` medium-grind cornmeal,
- `2 Tbsp` sugar,
- `2 tsp` baking powder,
- `1 1/2 tsp` salt,
- `1 1/4 cups` milk,
- `2 eggs`,
- `1/3 cup` oil or melted butter,
- baked in an `8"` square pan at `375 F`.

This house version keeps the same broad cornbread logic but adapts it to:

- standard white flour instead of the gluten-free flour blend,
- a larger `10"` cast-iron skillet,
- your fan-forced oven,
- olive oil as the default fat.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain white flour | 280 g | Default flour; better than high-protein flour for tenderness |
| Medium-grind cornmeal | 220 g | Bob's Red Mill medium grind |
| Sugar | 35 g | Enough to round the corn flavor without making it dessert-sweet |
| Baking powder | 11 g |  |
| Fine salt | 8 g |  |
| Milk | 400 g |  |
| Eggs | 3 large | About `150 g` out of shell |
| Olive oil | 80 g | Or melted butter |
| Extra oil or butter for skillet | as needed | For greasing and optional preheat |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to `190 C` fan-forced. Place the cast-iron skillet in the
   oven while it heats if you want a more assertive crust.
2. Lightly oil the hot or room-temperature skillet.
3. In a medium bowl combine the white flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder,
   and salt.
4. In a larger bowl whisk together the milk, eggs, and olive oil.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and whisk until just smooth.
   Do not overmix.
6. Rest the batter `5 to 10` minutes so the cornmeal can hydrate.
7. Pour or spoon the batter into the skillet and smooth the top.
8. Bake on the middle shelf for `28 to 35` minutes, until deeply golden and a
   toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean or with only a few moist
   crumbs.
9. Rest in the skillet for `10` minutes before slicing.

## Baking setup

- Tin, tray, skillet, or other vessel: `10"` cast-iron skillet with lid kept
  off
- Oven setting: fan-forced bake
- Temperature: `190 C`
- Time: `28 to 35` minutes

## Result targets

- Texture: moist, tender, and slightly crumbly rather than cakey
- Flavor: clear corn flavor with light sweetness
- Structure: slices cleanly while still feeling like cornbread
- Appearance: deep golden top and lightly crisp outer edge

## Variables to watch

- Whether your cornmeal wants the full `10` minute batter rest
- Whether the skillet is best preheated or merely greased cold
- Whether `190 C` fan gives the right balance between crust and crumb
- Whether you prefer a slightly lower sugar level for a more savory result

## Flour note

Default to ordinary white flour here, not the `21%` Vetta flour. Cornbread
usually benefits more from tenderness than from extra gluten strength. If you
want a slightly firmer slice, you could trial replacing up to `50 g` of the
white flour with the stronger flour, but that is not the house default.
