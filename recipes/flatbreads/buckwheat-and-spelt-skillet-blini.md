# Buckwheat and Spelt Skillet Blini

## Goal

Make a truly unleavened Russian-feeling flatbread by leaning into the quick
blini family: buckwheat-led, tender, buttery, and cooked as small skillet
breads rather than as a yeast-raised festival batter.

## Tradition note

Buckwheat blini sit very close to the heart of Russian bread culture. Historical
Russian blini were often yeast- or sourdough-based, but quick yeast-free blini
also existed, including richer versions made with butter, sour milk, and
beaten egg whites.

This house version follows that quick, unleavened branch. It keeps buckwheat
as the leading flour, uses wholemeal spelt as the support flour, and uses
Greek yogurt plus milk as the sour-milk analogue.

See `research/2026-04-07-unleavened-flatbreads-chickpea-quinoa-buckwheat.md`
for the source note behind this recipe.

## Modern adaptation choices

- Keeps the bread truly unleavened.
- Uses butter rather than olive oil because butter suits the blini tradition
  much better here.
- Uses spelt instead of plain white flour to keep the bread a little more rustic
  while still giving the buckwheat support.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buckwheat flour | 140 g | Main flour |
| Wholemeal spelt flour | 90 g | Support flour |
| Fine salt | 5 g |  |
| Greek yogurt | 180 g | Sour-milk stand-in |
| Milk | 220 g |  |
| Eggs | 2 large, separated | Separate whites and yolks |
| Butter | 25 g | Melted, plus more for the skillet |

## Method

1. In a bowl whisk together the buckwheat flour, spelt flour, and salt.
2. In a second bowl whisk the egg yolks, Greek yogurt, milk, and melted butter
   until smooth.
3. Whisk the wet mixture into the dry ingredients until no lumps remain.
4. Rest the batter `20` minutes so the flours hydrate.
5. Beat the egg whites to soft peaks and fold them gently into the rested
   batter.
6. Heat the cast-iron skillet over medium heat and butter it lightly.
7. Spoon in small rounds of batter, about `50 g` to `60 g` each. Cook `2` to
   `3` at a time depending on space.
8. Cook until the tops show bubbles and the edges look set, about `1 1/2` to
   `2` minutes.
9. Flip carefully and cook for a further `1` to `1 1/2` minutes.
10. Hold the finished blini under a cloth or in a low oven while you finish the
    batch.

## Cooking setup

- Vessel: lightly buttered cast-iron skillet
- Heat source: stovetop
- Heat level: medium
- Time: about `3` minutes per blini

## Result targets

- Texture: tender, pliable, and a little porous
- Flavor: nutty buckwheat with buttery, mildly tangy richness
- Structure: small flat breads that fold or roll without shattering
- Appearance: browned spots with a warm grey-brown interior

## Variables to watch

- Whether the batter wants a little extra milk after resting
- Whether the `140/90` buckwheat-to-spelt split feels right
- Whether you prefer smaller, thicker blini or slightly larger, thinner ones

## Notes

- This recipe is still unleavened even though it uses whipped egg whites.
- If the first blini tears, lower the heat slightly and make the next ones a
  little smaller.
- Very good with butter, smoked fish, yogurt, sour cream, or jam.
