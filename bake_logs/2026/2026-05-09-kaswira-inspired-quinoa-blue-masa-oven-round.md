# 2026-05-09 Kaswira-Inspired Quinoa and Blue Masa Oven Round

## What was made

A first trial of the single-round oven-baked version of the kaswira-inspired
quinoa and blue masa flatbread, pressed into the cast-iron skillet and baked in
the oven rather than cooked as small stovetop griddle breads.

## Starting point

- Recipe version: informal first-pass guidance from 2026-05-08
- Batch size: about `250 g` total flour
- Planned shape: one round skillet "pancake"
- Planned baking method: cast-iron skillet in the oven, with grill finish

## What changed this time

- Followed the proposed single-skillet-round approach.
- Finished with about `5` minutes under the grill to brown the top.

## Observations during mixing and dough development

- Dough was workable enough to press into a single round.
- Hydration was probably a little too high for the thickness and oven method.

## Observations during bulk fermentation or resting

- Not applicable: unleavened bread.
- Resting helped the quinoa flour and blue masa hydrate, but the final dough
  still carried enough water to remain pasty in the centre after baking.

## Observations during shaping and final proof

- The single-round shape was manageable in the skillet.
- No transfer into hot iron was needed, which kept handling calm.

## Observations during baking

- Top and bottom browned well.
- The `5` minute grill finish gave strong surface colour.
- Interior did not dry and set as much as the exterior suggested.

## Outcome

- Crumb: somewhere between gummy and pasty in the centre.
- Crust: well browned top and bottom.
- Flavor: delicious despite the texture problem.
- Structure: held together, but centre texture was not quite right.
- Overall success: tasty and promising, but not yet the target method.

## Human judgement

Worth repeating, but with less water and perhaps a slightly more masa-forward
ratio so the centre sets more cleanly.

## Changes for next time

- Hold back about `15 g` water.
- Try a `2:1` quinoa-to-blue-masa ratio.
- Consider seasoning the dough if treating it more like a savoury biscuit-style
  skillet bread.
