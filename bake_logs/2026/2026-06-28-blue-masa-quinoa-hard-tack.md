## Observations during baking

- Residual-heat oven drying drove the biscuits very dry.
- The result appears to have replicated true hard tack using a much faster
  baking route than traditional multi-hour drying methods.

## Outcome

- Crumb: very dry and hard.
- Crust: hard and thoroughly dried.
- Flavor: superb.
- Structure: successful hard-tack structure rather than a merely crisp cracker.
- Overall success: strong success for true hard-tack texture and flavour.

## Human judgement

The blue masa and quinoa variant was superb, but very hard. Final moisture was
not measured, but the baker estimated it was probably no more than about `10%`.

## Changes for next time

- Decide whether this variant should remain the true hard-tack benchmark.
- For a more palatable version, reduce the residual-heat drying time.
- For a lighter "puffed tack" direction, trial `10 g` to `12 g` baking powder
  per `300 g` flour, treating `15 g` as the upper experimental end.
