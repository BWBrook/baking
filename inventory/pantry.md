# Pantry Baseline

This file records non-flour baking staples currently on hand.

## Leavening

| Item | Current role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instant dry yeast | Default yeast leavener | Current standard for yeasted doughs. |
| Baking powder | Default chemical leavener | Current standard for chemically leavened baking. |
| Baking soda | Secondary chemical leavener | Available for future use with acidic ingredients or cream of tartar. |

## Refrigerated and fresh ingredients

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh farm eggs | General baking use. |
| Greek yogurt | Useful for enriched doughs, cakes, soda-style bakes, or moisture. |

## Fats

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Olive oil | Preferred default fat where suitable. |
| Butter | Available when a recipe clearly benefits from butter. |

## Current practice notes

- For yeasted breads, assume instant dry yeast unless the recipe says
  otherwise.
- For chemically leavened bakes, assume baking powder unless a more specific
  acid-base approach is being tested.
- Keep future space open for sourdough starter, levain, and soda plus cream of
  tartar workflows.
